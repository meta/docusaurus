## `new URL()` images

Those always render: images referenced with `new URL()` are not transformed by the IdealImage and SVGR plugins.

- Relative path: <img id="new-url-relative" alt="OSS logo" src={new URL('./img/oss_logo.png', import.meta.url).href} />
- Relative path with spaces: <img id="new-url-spaces" alt="OSS logo" src={new URL('./img/oss_logo (2).png', import.meta.url).href} />
- `@site` alias: <img id="new-url-site-alias" alt="Docusaurus social card" width={300} src={new URL('@site/static/img/docusaurus-social-card.png', import.meta.url).href} />
- SVG: <img id="new-url-svg" alt="Docusaurus logo" width={100} src={new URL('@site/static/img/docusaurus.svg', import.meta.url).href} />
- IdealImage: <Image img={new URL('@site/static/img/docusaurus.png', import.meta.url).href} />
